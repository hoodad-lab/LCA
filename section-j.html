Where colour counts in Section J

Roofs: a pass or fail limit

In the deemed-to-satisfy route (NCC 2022, J4D4) the upper surface of a roof must have a solar absorptance of no more than 0.45, which is a TSR of at least 55%. This applies in most climate zones; check the clause for your zone and any exemptions.

Walls: an input for the modeller

The deemed-to-satisfy wall rules are about insulation and glazing, not colour. Where the building is energy modelled (the verification route), the modeller enters each facade's solar absorptance. A lower value means less heat soaking into the wall and less cooling.

Dark colours on insulated facades

Not a Section J rule, but it matters for the same colour choice. For external insulation systems (ETICS/EIFS), dark colours need a TSR of at least 25% to stay thermally safe (VDPM guidance). KEIM Coolit has a TSR of 30 or more in every shade.

Which NCC edition?

These limits are from NCC 2022 Volume One. NCC 2025 is being adopted state by state from 2026 to 2027, with different timing in each state. Check which edition applies to your project with the building certifier.
