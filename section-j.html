    Where colour counts in Section J

    Walls: an input for the modeller

    The deemed-to-satisfy wall rules are about insulation and glazing, not colour. Where the building is energy modelled (J1V3), the modeller enters each facade's solar absorptance. Coolit's lower value means less heat gain in the model.

    Insulated facades need TSR 25+

    Section J keeps raising insulation levels, so more buildings use external insulation. A dark colour on insulation overheats unless its TSR is 25 or more (VDPM). KEIM Coolit has a TSR of 30 or more in every shade.

    Roofs: a pass or fail limit

    In the deemed-to-satisfy route (NCC 2022, J4D4) a roof's upper surface must have a solar absorptance of 0.45 or less in most climate zones. This applies to roof finishes rather than facade paints.
